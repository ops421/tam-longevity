FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to common questions about our treatments, process, and care.

Are semaglutide and tirzepatide FDA-approved?

Certain brand-name medications that contain semaglutide and tirzepatide are FDA-approved for specific uses. Compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide are not FDA-approved, and the FDA does not review compounded medications for safety, effectiveness, or quality before they are marketed. Compounded medications are prepared by a licensed pharmacy for an individual patient based on a valid prescription.

Who can take compounded semaglutide or compounded tirzepatide?

Only a licensed healthcare provider can determine whether treatment is appropriate for you, after reviewing your medical history, current medications, and health goals. These medications are not right for everyone, including people with a personal or family history of medullary thyroid carcinoma (MTC) or Multiple Endocrine Neoplasia syndrome type 2 (MEN 2).

How is the medication prescribed?

You complete a secure online health assessment. A licensed healthcare provider reviews your information and, if treatment is appropriate, issues a prescription written for you.

Where is my medication shipped from?

If prescribed, your medication is dispensed by a licensed U.S. pharmacy partner, including TAM Pharmacy and Epiq Scripts, and shipped to your address where permitted by law. Typical pharmacy processing is 24 to 48 hours after prescription approval; carrier delivery times vary.

How much does it cost?

Illustrative pricing is $135 per month with a 6-month plan, $160 per month with a 3-month plan, $179 per month with a 1-month plan. Final pricing is confirmed with you before you are charged. No insurance is required.

What kind of support will I receive during treatment?

Your care team is available for treatment-related questions and follow-up care throughout your program, and your provider can adjust your plan as needed.
