1. Introduction and Scope

TAM Longevity LLC ("TAM Longevity," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, retain, and protect personal information when you visit our websites, complete assessments, create an account, purchase or request services, communicate with us, or otherwise use our online services (collectively, the "Services").

TAM Longevity facilitates access to healthcare-related services, including online intake, care coordination, and, where appropriate, prescription fulfillment through independent licensed healthcare professionals and pharmacies. Treatment availability is subject to medical evaluation and applicable law. This policy applies to information TAM Longevity handles in connection with its Services. Separate privacy notices may apply to information handled directly by a prescribing medical practice, healthcare provider, pharmacy, laboratory, or other independent organization.

2. Who May Use the Services

The Services are intended for adults age 18 and older, unless a specific service expressly states otherwise and appropriate consent is obtained. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13 through our general Services. If you believe a child provided information to us, contact us using the information below.

3. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect:

  • Identity and contact details, including name, date of birth, address, email, phone number, and account identifiers.
  • Account and transaction details, including order history, subscriptions, billing address, payment status, and communications preferences. Payment-card details may be processed by payment service providers rather than stored by TAM Longevity.
  • Health-related information you choose to submit, including medical history, current medications, allergies, symptoms, treatment interests, intake responses, and related documents or communications.
  • Prescription and fulfillment information necessary to coordinate with prescribing professionals, pharmacies, and delivery providers.
  • Customer-service communications, including emails, messages, call records or recordings where lawful and disclosed, and requests for assistance.
  • Technical and usage information, such as IP address, browser and device information, pages viewed, referring pages, cookie identifiers, and approximate location derived from technical information.
  • Information from healthcare providers, pharmacies, service providers, and other parties when permitted by law and necessary to deliver the Services.

We do not necessarily collect every category listed above from every user. Certain data, including health information, may be considered sensitive under state privacy laws.

4. How We Use Information

We use information as necessary and permitted by applicable law to:

  • Operate our website and accounts, respond to inquiries, and provide customer support.
  • Facilitate medical intake, eligibility review, telehealth appointments, care coordination, prescription processing, and pharmacy fulfillment.
  • Process payments, administer subscriptions and orders, and send transaction and shipping updates.
  • Verify identity, maintain security, detect fraud, and protect users and our Services.
  • Improve website functionality, understand general service performance, and maintain records.
  • Send service-related messages and, where permitted and subject to your choices, marketing communications.
  • Meet legal, regulatory, licensing, recordkeeping, and contractual obligations; establish or defend legal claims.

We will obtain consent when required by applicable law, including for certain uses or disclosures of sensitive or consumer health data. We do not use medical intake responses or protected medical records for targeted advertising unless a lawful basis and any required specific authorization or consent are in place.

5. Medical Information, HIPAA, and Independent Providers

TAM Longevity LLC acts as a business associate in connection with certain services performed for HIPAA-covered healthcare providers or other business associates. When TAM Longevity creates, receives, maintains, or transmits protected health information (PHI) in that capacity, it handles that PHI in accordance with HIPAA, applicable business associate agreements, and other relevant law. HIPAA-protected PHI is not used or disclosed for advertising or marketing except where specifically permitted by applicable law and contractual obligations, including any required authorization.

Not all information collected on a public website is necessarily PHI under HIPAA. Information collected outside our business associate activities may instead be subject to federal or state consumer privacy or consumer health data laws. Whether a particular data item is PHI depends on the facts and our role when handling it.

Independent prescribing practices, clinicians, and pharmacies may provide their own Notices of Privacy Practices. Their notices govern their covered activities and patients' HIPAA rights. This website Privacy Policy does not replace those notices. To request access to or correction of medical records held by an independent provider or pharmacy, contact that organization or call us for assistance identifying the appropriate contact.

Please do not send medical records or sensitive health details through ordinary email (including info@tamlongevity.com), social media messages, or marketing text-message replies. Use approved secure patient channels when available.

6. When We Disclose Information

We may disclose information, subject to applicable law and appropriate safeguards, to:

  • Licensed healthcare providers, medical groups, telehealth networks, pharmacies, and laboratories involved in evaluating, prescribing, dispensing, or coordinating your care.
  • Technology, hosting, payment, identity verification, customer support, messaging, fulfillment, analytics, and security vendors that provide services to us.
  • Shipping carriers and delivery services to fulfill orders.
  • Professional advisers, auditors, insurers, regulators, law enforcement, or courts when required or permitted by law.
  • Parties involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, or sale of assets, subject to applicable privacy protections.
  • Other parties when you direct us to disclose information or provide legally valid consent.

We require service providers to handle information in accordance with applicable legal and contractual requirements. We do not sell PHI or medical records. We use third-party advertising and analytics tracking on our public website, which may involve sharing cookie identifiers, device information, and browsing activity with advertising technology partners. Depending on applicable state law and how the technologies are configured, these disclosures may be treated as a sale, sharing, or targeted advertising, even when no money changes hands. We will provide legally required notice, consent mechanisms, and opt-out choices. We will not intentionally transmit PHI, medical intake answers, prescription details, or other sensitive health information to advertising platforms. Tracking on health-related pages must be evaluated for potential disclosure of consumer health data or PHI.

7. Cookies, Analytics, and Advertising

Our website uses cookies, pixels, tags, and similar technologies for functionality, analytics, advertising measurement, and marketing. These tools may collect IP addresses, device and browser details, advertising identifiers, pages viewed, referring URLs, and interactions with our website. Advertising partners may use permitted information to measure campaigns or show ads on other sites or services.

Health-related page visits and inferred treatment interests can be sensitive under applicable law. We will configure tracking to avoid collecting or transmitting patient intake responses, medical records, prescription information, and other PHI or sensitive health details for advertising purposes. Where required by law, we will obtain consent before enabling nonessential technologies, offer an opt-out of sale/sharing or targeted advertising, and honor applicable opt-out preference signals such as Global Privacy Control.

Until a website privacy-preferences link is configured, you may contact us at 888-423-5988, at info@tamlongevity.com, or by mail to request information about available privacy choices. 8. Email, SMS, and Other Communications

We may send administrative and service messages relating to accounts, appointments, prescriptions, orders, shipping, security, and customer support. We also send promotional email and SMS messages to people who have opted in or where otherwise permitted by law. Marketing messages may describe TAM Longevity services, treatment categories, educational materials, or promotions. Consent to marketing texts is not a condition of purchase or receiving medical care.

You may unsubscribe from promotional emails using the unsubscribe link included in those emails. You may opt out of promotional SMS messages by replying STOP to the sending number, where supported, by calling 888-423-5988, or by emailing info@tamlongevity.com. Reply HELP where supported for assistance. Message frequency varies; message and data rates may apply. Opting out of promotional communications will not necessarily stop important nonmarketing service messages permitted by law. We do not use PHI for marketing without any authorization required under HIPAA, and we do not include sensitive medical details in promotional messages without appropriate authorization and safeguards. SMS and ordinary email may not be secure channels for medical information.

9. Data Retention

We retain personal information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including providing Services, meeting applicable medical and pharmacy recordkeeping requirements, resolving disputes, preventing fraud, and complying with law. Retention periods vary by information category and applicable legal obligations. When information is no longer required, we take reasonable steps to delete, de-identify, or securely dispose of it.

10. Information Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards appropriate to the nature of the information we handle. No website, electronic communication, or data storage system is completely secure. You are responsible for safeguarding your account credentials and promptly reporting suspected unauthorized access.

Depending on your state of residence and the law that applies, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or a copy of certain personal information; to opt out of certain targeted advertising, sales, or profiling; to limit or withdraw consent for certain uses of sensitive or consumer health data; and to appeal certain privacy-rights decisions.

You may submit a privacy request by calling 888-423-5988, emailing info@tamlongevity.com, or writing to the mailing address below. We will update this policy if an online request form is established. Medical-record access or amendment requests relating to a particular healthcare provider or pharmacy may need to be directed to that organization under its Notice of Privacy Practices.
