About TAM Longevity

What is TAM Longevity?

TAM Longevity is an online healthcare platform that connects patients with licensed medical providers for personalized medical evaluations, treatment options, and ongoing care.

When medically appropriate, prescription medications may be dispensed and shipped directly to patients by licensed U.S. pharmacies.

How does TAM Longevity work?

Getting started is simple:

  1. Complete Your Health Assessment: Provide information about your medical history, current medications, and health concerns.
  2. Connect With a Provider: A licensed healthcare provider reviews your information and determines whether treatment is appropriate.
  3. Receive Your Treatment Plan: Your provider discusses available options, potential risks, and recommended follow-up care.
  4. Receive Your Medication: If prescribed, medication is dispensed by a licensed pharmacy and shipped where permitted by law.
What types of treatments does TAM Longevity offer?

TAM Longevity provides access to medical evaluations and treatment options in select areas of health and wellness.

Available treatments are listed on our website and may change as additional services become available.

All prescription treatments require evaluation and authorization by a licensed healthcare provider.

Do I need to visit a doctor's office?

Many patients can complete their medical evaluation online.

Depending on your state, medical history, and treatment needs, your provider may require a video consultation, telephone consultation, additional testing, or an in-person examination.

Do I need a prescription?

Prescription medications require authorization from a licensed healthcare provider.

Your provider will review your medical information and determine whether treatment is appropriate. Completing an assessment does not guarantee a prescription.

Do I need health insurance?

Health insurance is not required to use TAM Longevity.

You can review applicable pricing and payment terms before completing your purchase.

How much does treatment cost?

Pricing depends on the treatment or service selected.

Applicable charges and payment terms are displayed during the enrollment or checkout process before you authorize payment.

Medications and Medical Care

How do I know which treatment is right for me?

A licensed healthcare provider will evaluate your medical history, current medications, health concerns, and other relevant information.

Your provider will determine which treatment options, if any, are medically appropriate.

Are all medications FDA-approved?

No. TAM Longevity may offer access to both FDA-approved and compounded prescription medications, depending on availability and medical eligibility.

Compounded medications are not FDA-approved. The FDA does not review compounded medications for safety, effectiveness, or quality before marketing.

The regulatory status of a medication will be identified in its product information.

Can my dosage change during treatment?

Your provider may adjust your prescribed dosage based on your medical needs, response to treatment, and other clinical considerations.

Do not change your dosage or treatment schedule without instructions from your prescribing provider.

What if I experience side effects?

Contact your prescribing provider or care team if you experience side effects or have questions about your medication.

Your provider can evaluate your concerns and determine whether changes to your treatment are necessary.

For a medical emergency, seek immediate emergency medical attention.

Can I request a refill?

Refills depend on your prescription, medication, and any required follow-up evaluation.

Your care team can explain the refill process and whether additional medical information is needed.

Can I contact my provider during treatment?

Patients have access to their care team for treatment-related questions and follow-up care.

Your care team will provide instructions for contacting your provider or requesting additional medical assistance.

Shipping and Medication Handling

How long does it take to receive my medication?

Our typical pharmacy processing turnaround is 24 to 48 hours after a prescription has been approved and received for fulfillment.

Actual delivery times depend on pharmacy processing, medication availability, shipping schedules, carrier transit, and destination.

Shipping estimates are not guaranteed.

How is my medication shipped?

Medications are packaged according to their specific storage and transportation requirements.

Certain medications require temperature-controlled packaging, while others may be shipped without refrigeration.

Your shipment will include applicable medication storage and handling instructions.

My package arrived and the ice packs were thawed. Is my medication still safe to use?

Ice packs used in temperature-controlled shipments are designed to help regulate the temperature inside the package. They are not necessarily expected to remain frozen throughout transit.

A thawed ice pack does not automatically mean that your medication has been compromised.

However, medication stability depends on the specific product, packaging, transit duration, and temperatures experienced during shipping.

If your medication arrives with thawed ice packs, refrigerate it promptly if directed by its storage instructions and contact the dispensing pharmacy if you have any concerns.

Do not use medication if you suspect it has been exposed to temperatures outside its permitted range until the dispensing pharmacy confirms that it is appropriate to use.

What if my package is delayed?

If your shipment is delayed, check the tracking information provided with your order.

For medications requiring refrigeration, contact the dispensing pharmacy if the shipment exceeds its expected transit window or you have concerns about storage conditions.

The pharmacy can provide product-specific guidance on whether the medication remains suitable for use.

What if my medication arrives damaged or incorrect?

Contact our support team within 48 hours of delivery if your medication arrives damaged, defective, or incorrect.

Please retain the medication and original packaging and provide photographs when requested.

Our team will coordinate with the dispensing pharmacy to review the issue and determine the appropriate resolution.

Do not use medication that appears damaged, contaminated, or incorrectly labeled.

Billing, Cancellations and Refunds

What is your refund policy?

Prescription medications are prepared and dispensed for individual patients and generally cannot be returned or refunded once an order has been sent to the pharmacy for fulfillment.

Orders may be canceled before they are submitted to the pharmacy for filling.

Refund requests involving damaged, defective, or incorrect medication are reviewed individually.

Please see our full Return, Refund and Subscription Policy for additional details.

Can I cancel my order?

You may request cancellation before your order has been submitted to the pharmacy for filling.

Once an order has been submitted to the pharmacy, cancellation may no longer be possible.

If cancellation is approved before pharmacy fulfillment begins, eligible payments will be refunded to the original payment method.

What if my provider determines I am not eligible for treatment?

If your provider determines that prescription treatment is not medically appropriate, no medication will be dispensed.

Any refund eligibility for payments already collected will be governed by the terms disclosed at checkout and our Refund Policy.

Can I return unused medication?

For safety and regulatory reasons, prescription medications generally cannot be returned after dispensing.

If you receive incorrect, damaged, or defective medication, contact our support team for assistance.

How do I request a refund?

Contact TAM Longevity customer support with your order number, purchase date, and a description of the issue.

Supporting photographs or additional documentation may be requested.

Refund requests are reviewed individually in accordance with our Refund Policy.

Will my subscription automatically renew?

If you enroll in a subscription or recurring treatment program, the renewal schedule, payment amount, and cancellation terms will be disclosed before enrollment.

You can review your subscription terms and available cancellation options through the applicable patient account or support channel.
