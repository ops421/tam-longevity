Return, Refund and Subscription Policy

Last Updated: November 9, 2026

1. Overview

TAM Longevity LLC ("TAM Longevity," "we," "us," or "our") provides an online platform facilitating access to healthcare-related services, medical evaluations, and, where clinically appropriate, prescription products dispensed by appropriately licensed pharmacies. This Return, Refund and Subscription Policy explains how requests for cancellation, refunds, returns, billing corrections, and shipment issues are handled. Treatment and prescription eligibility are determined by licensed healthcare providers, not guaranteed by payment or completion of an intake form.

2. Pricing and Checkout Disclosures

Before you authorize a payment, the applicable checkout process will identify the amount due, what is included, any separately charged medical or administrative fees, shipping charges, recurring billing terms, and any commitment period. Prices, discounts, and services vary by product or program. The terms disclosed at checkout for a particular order or plan form part of that purchase, subject to applicable law.

3. Prescription Products and Returns

For patient safety and applicable pharmacy handling requirements, prescription medications that have been dispensed generally cannot be returned for resale or reuse. A change of mind, dissatisfaction with results, or unused medication does not automatically qualify for a refund after dispensing. This does not eliminate remedies required by law or the remedies for confirmed damaged, defective, incorrect, or unfulfilled orders described below. The dispensing pharmacy may have additional handling requirements.

4. Initial Orders and Medical Eligibility

A prescription is not guaranteed. If a licensed provider determines that treatment is not medically appropriate and no prescription is issued, the disposition of any authorization hold, medication charge, or separately disclosed service fee will follow the terms shown at checkout and applicable law. We will not represent a temporary bank authorization as a completed refund; release timing may depend on the financial institution.

5. Cancelling an Initial Order

Contact TAM Longevity promptly if you wish to cancel an order. A request received before pharmacy fulfillment begins may be eligible for cancellation and refund of amounts not yet earned or incurred, as disclosed at checkout and required by law. Once a prescription has been transmitted for dispensing or a pharmacy has begun preparing or dispensing an individualized order, cancellation or refund may no longer be possible.

6. Subscriptions and Automatic Renewal

Some programs may be offered on a recurring basis. If you choose a subscription, we will disclose the recurring price, billing frequency, renewal terms, any minimum commitment, and cancellation method before enrollment. You authorize recurring charges only as described in the terms you accept. Subscription cancellation stops future renewals as specified at checkout; it does not necessarily reverse charges for services already rendered or medication already dispensed. We will provide cancellation options and notices as required by applicable law.

7. Multi-Month or Prepaid Plans

If offered, a multi-month plan may involve an upfront commitment with medication dispensed in installments. The total price, number of shipments, delivery cadence, renewal status, and refund terms must be displayed before purchase. Cancellation of automatic renewal does not automatically cancel an already-purchased term. No unapproved nonrefundability rule is adopted by this draft.

8. Medically Directed Discontinuation and Unavailable Products

If a licensed provider determines that treatment should stop, or if an ordered product cannot legally or practically be fulfilled, TAM Longevity will review unfulfilled portions of the order in coordination with the provider and dispensing pharmacy. Any alternative treatment requires an appropriate medical evaluation and patient agreement. Refunds or credits for undelivered goods or services will be handled under the applicable checkout terms and law.

9. Shipping and Fulfillment

Our typical pharmacy processing turnaround is 24 to 48 hours after prescription approval and receipt for fulfillment, subject to pharmacy schedules and product availability. This is a processing estimate, not a guarantee that a package will arrive within 24 to 48 hours. Transit time depends on the carrier, destination, weather, and other circumstances. Patients must provide a correct, deliverable shipping address and promptly review tracking notices.

10. Temperature-Sensitive Shipments

Some medications are shipped with temperature-control packaging. Ice packs are designed to buffer temperature during transit and may be thawed upon arrival. A thawed ice pack alone does not establish that a medication is unusable. However, whether a specific product remains suitable depends on that product's validated shipping conditions, package configuration, and transit history. Follow the dispensing pharmacy's storage instructions, refrigerate promptly when directed, and contact the pharmacy before using medication if a shipment is delayed or you suspect an excursion beyond permitted conditions. This policy does not make a blanket claim that every product remains safe for 48 hours.

11. Damaged, Defective, Incorrect, or Missing Orders

Please inspect deliveries promptly. Report damaged packaging, suspected defects, incorrect medication, missing items, or other delivery problems as soon as possible, preferably within 48 hours of delivery, by calling 888-423-5988 or emailing info@tamlongevity.com. Keep the original packaging, medication, and photographs of the shipment and label. Do not use medication that appears incorrect, compromised, or damaged until the dispensing pharmacy advises you. TAM Longevity will coordinate review with the dispensing pharmacy and carrier, as applicable. A verified issue may qualify for correction, replacement, or refund depending on the circumstances and applicable law. The 48-hour reporting request does not waive any rights that cannot legally be waived.

12. Lost, Delayed, or Misdelivered Packages

If tracking shows a package has not been delivered, contact TAM Longevity so the shipment can be investigated with the carrier and pharmacy. If tracking shows delivery but you did not receive the package, notify us promptly. Reshipment or refund decisions will depend on the investigation, the applicable terms, and law. Incorrect addresses, unapproved address changes, refused deliveries, or theft after delivery may affect eligibility, but no categorical waiver of statutory rights is intended.

13. Billing Errors and Approved Refunds

If you believe you were charged incorrectly, including a duplicate charge or an unauthorized renewal, contact us with the order details. We will investigate and correct verified billing errors. Approved refunds generally return to the original payment method, unless otherwise required or agreed. Financial institutions may require additional time to post a refund.

14. Chargebacks and Payment Disputes

You may contact TAM Longevity first to help resolve a billing concern, but this policy does not restrict any lawful right to dispute a charge with your card issuer or financial institution. We may review account activity to investigate payment disputes in accordance with law.

15. Policy Changes

We may revise this policy to reflect operational or legal changes. Material changes affecting recurring billing, cancellation, or refund rights will be communicated as required by law. Changes do not retroactively override terms already accepted for a transaction where prohibited by law.

16. Legal Rights

Federal and state consumer protection, automatic renewal, telehealth, and pharmacy laws may provide additional rights. If a provision conflicts with applicable nonwaivable law, that law controls. Nothing in this policy limits rights or remedies that cannot legally be waived.
